Welcome to the Stars Hollow Scavenger Hunt!

All around the museum are hiddedn QR codes for you to scan with your phone to unlock a word. Once you have enough words you can solve our puzzle!

Make sure you allow cookies and aren't in incognito/privacy mode as a default. Chrome browser works best.
